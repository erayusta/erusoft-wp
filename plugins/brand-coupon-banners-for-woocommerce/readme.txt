Shows scheduled, brand- or category-scoped coupon banners (image or text) on shop archives and matching product pages.

== Description ==

Create banners for coupon campaigns scoped to a brand taxonomy (selectable in settings) and/or product categories, with start/end dates, desktop and mobile images or a coloured text banner. Banners appear on shop, category and brand archives and on matching product pages (classic and block themes). A WooCommerce coupon can be created automatically (restricted to the chosen categories). Filter: `eru_bcb_banner`.

Diğer Erusoft eklentileri: https://wordpress.erusoft.com

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate it (WooCommerce must be active).
3. Configure it under Marka Bannerları → Ayarlar.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it output anything with no banners? =
No. Nothing is added to the front end until an active banner matches.

= What is removed on uninstall? =
Its own settings (`eru_bcb_*` options). Banner posts (`eru_bcb_banner`), their meta and coupons are kept.
